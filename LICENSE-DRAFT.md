DRAFT ONLY. This document is a proposed license and grants no rights until the
owner approves and publishes a final LICENSE. It is not MIT and is not an
OSI-approved open-source license. Legal review is recommended before adoption.

## Proposed business terms

1. Users may download, inspect, install and run the official CLI to analyze
   software they own or are authorized to work on. This includes ordinary
   business use by companies, paid development work and internal CI.
2. Users may make private modifications for that same internal use and may
   submit proposed changes back to the official project.
3. Without separate written permission from the rights holder, users may not
   sell, license, rebrand, sublicense, embed or distribute the CLI or modified
   versions as part of a commercial offering. They may not provide its analysis
   functionality to third parties as a paid/monetized service or as a feature
   of a commercial product. Keeping a wrapper free does not exempt it when
   it supports such an offering.
4. Running the CLI as a development tool does not license or restrict the
   user's application or its outputs. Delivering normal development work to
   a client is permitted; selling CLI-derived analysis functionality is not.
5. Public distribution of modified builds requires separate permission. Source
   hosting platform forks for inspection and contributions, and rights required
   by the platform terms, remain permitted; they do not authorize commercial
   redistribution or operation of a commercial derivative.
6. Copyright/attribution and third-party notices must remain intact. Third-party
   components retain their own licenses; these restrictions apply only to
   original Genesis code owned by the licensor.
7. The license grants no rights to private server implementation, provider
   credentials, hosted-service access, or Genesis names/logos as product branding.
   Hosted access uses separate credentials and service terms.

Before adoption, confirm the actual rights-holding entity, contact for commercial
permission, warranty/liability language and termination/remediation provisions.
This draft is intentionally not a completed legal instrument.
